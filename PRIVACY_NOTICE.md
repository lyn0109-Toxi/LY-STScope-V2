# Privacy Notice

LY-STScope Ver.2 is currently designed as an educational Streamlit prototype.

## Current Prototype Behavior

- The app is intended for learning, demonstration, and analytical discussion.
- User-entered portfolio, personal finance, diary, and comment data are handled
  as session-based prototype inputs.
- The current prototype should not be used to collect or store sensitive
  personal financial information.

## Do Not Enter Sensitive Information

Users should not enter confidential personal data, including:

- Social Security numbers or tax IDs
- Bank account numbers
- Credit card numbers
- Passwords or API keys
- Full legal identity documents
- Highly sensitive personal financial records

## Future Production Requirements

If LY-STScope later becomes a production personal finance or asset management
service, it should add:

- A formal privacy policy
- User authentication and authorization
- Encrypted data storage
- Data deletion and export controls
- Clear consent language
- Security review and monitoring
- Compliance review for financial, privacy, and consumer protection rules

## No Advice

LY-STScope does not provide financial, investment, legal, tax, accounting, or
professional advice. Users are responsible for verifying all information and
consulting qualified professionals before making financial decisions.
